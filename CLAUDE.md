# Dungeon Climb

Retro pixel endless climber (PICO-8 style palette, 144x256 canvas).
Single file: index.html, vanilla JS + Canvas, no dependencies.
Live: https://itsjustaii.github.io/dungeon-climb

## Gameplay
- Explorer runs out of a cave, lava pours in, he climbs automatically
- 3 vines (lanes), swipe left/right to switch (Subway Surfers style)
- Broken vines = fall and die; fragile vines (brown) snap 2s after grabbing
- Falling spikes (! warning) and wall darts stun you, lava catches up
- Score = height + close call bonuses (CLOSE / DODGE / CLUTCH), combos
