# Dungeon Climb

A retro pixel endless climber. Lava is rising. Climb or burn.

▶ **Play:** https://itsjustaii.github.io/dungeon-climb

## How to play
- Your explorer climbs automatically
- **Swipe left / right** to switch vines (or **← →** / **A D** on keyboard)
- Tap or press **Space** to start and retry

## Watch out for
- **Broken vines**: climb into a gap and you fall
- **Fragile vines** (brown): you get 2 seconds before they snap
- **Falling spikes**: a `!` warns which lane
- **Darts**: wall traps blink, then fire at your lane

Hits stun you and let the lava catch up.

## Scoring
Height + bonus points for close calls:
- **CLOSE**: dodge a spike or dart at the last second
- **DODGE**: leave a vine right before it breaks
- **CLUTCH**: jump off a fragile vine just before it snaps

Chain them for combos (x2, x3...).

## Tech
Single HTML file, vanilla JavaScript + Canvas, no dependencies.
